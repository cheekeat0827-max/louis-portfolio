About Me

Turning client briefs into functional, good-looking websites.

I'm a Web Programmer with hands-on experience developing, maintaining and customizing responsive websites using WordPress, Elementor, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP. I build user-friendly sites, customize layouts and functionality, troubleshoot front-end issues, and optimize every project across desktop, tablet and mobile.

Beyond the build, I handle WooCommerce stores end-to-end — product pages, categories, variations and cart flows — plus plugin integrations (forms, WhatsApp chat, job listings, 360° virtual tours) and ongoing maintenance, content updates and basic on-page SEO.
